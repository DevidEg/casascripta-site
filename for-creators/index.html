THE TERMS

The terms, in plain English.

The split

Every sale through your link is split 60/40 in your favour: you receive 60 percent, Casa Scripta 40 percent. The split is on what the customer paid, not counting sales tax. Gumroad's fees are shared in the same 60/40 proportion.

No cost to you

You do not pay a setup fee, a monthly fee, or a fee for the writing or design. If the book does not sell, you owe nothing.

You approve the final version

The ebook will not go out under your name until you have approved the final version in writing. An email counts as writing.

Checkout and delivery

The book is sold on Gumroad, which acts as merchant of record, collects sales tax where it must, and delivers the file to the buyer.

How you are paid

You need your own Gumroad account, with payment settings filled in. We add you as a Gumroad affiliate on your book at 60 percent, and you share it with your own link. Every sale through your link pays 60 percent straight into your Gumroad balance, and Gumroad pays it out on the schedule you choose there. Casa Scripta never holds your money. Gumroad's fees are shared in the same 60/40 proportion, and we send you a short sales report by the 15th of each month.

You can leave

Either side can end the partnership by giving 30 days' written notice. An email counts. Your audience stays yours. When it ends, your name comes off the book and Casa Scripta stops using it to sell. You keep your share of every sale made before the end date.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this exclusive? No. You can promote other products, and Casa Scripta can work with other creators and sell other ebooks.

Who owns the book? Casa Scripta wrote and designed the ebook, so it holds the copyright in the text and design and keeps the book on sale. Your name, face, brand and audience stay yours, and nothing in the partnership changes that. While we work together you can promote the book with its cover, excerpts and your sales link. When the partnership ends, your name comes off the book.

Do I have to post a certain amount? No. You promote the book in whatever way, and as often, as you choose.

What if a sale is refunded? If a sale through your link is refunded, Gumroad takes back the commission on that sale from your balance, so a refund is shared 60/40, the same way as the sale.

Do you promise a certain income? No. Sales depend on whether your audience wants the book. There is no minimum and no guarantee.
