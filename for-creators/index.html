THE TERMS

The terms, in plain English.

The split

Every sale is split 60/40 in your favour: you receive 60 percent of net sales, Casa Scripta 40 percent. Net sales means what the customer paid, minus sales tax, Gumroad's fees and refunds.

No cost to you

You do not pay a setup fee, a monthly fee, or a fee for the writing or design. If the book does not sell, you owe nothing.

You approve the final version

The ebook will not go out under your name until you have approved the final version in writing. An email counts as writing.

Checkout and delivery

The book is sold on Gumroad, which acts as merchant of record, collects sales tax where it must, and delivers the file to the buyer.

How you are paid

You need your own Gumroad account, with payment settings filled in. Gumroad can pay a collaborator automatically, up to a maximum cut of 50 percent. We set that cut at 50 percent, so half of each sale goes straight into your Gumroad balance and is paid out on the schedule you choose there. Casa Scripta does not handle that money. The remaining 10 percent, which brings your share to 60, is paid to you by Casa Scripta with a sales report by the 15th of the following month, covering the previous calendar month.

You can leave

Either side can end the partnership by giving 30 days' written notice. An email counts. Your audience stays yours. When it ends, your name comes off the book and Casa Scripta stops using it to sell. You keep your share of every sale made before the end date.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this exclusive? No. You can promote other products, and Casa Scripta can work with other creators and sell other ebooks.

Who owns the book? Casa Scripta owns the ebook, including its text and design. While we work together you have a licence to promote it, including the cover, excerpts and sales links. That licence ends when the partnership ends.

Do I have to post a certain amount? No. You promote the book in whatever way, and as often, as you choose.

What if a sale is refunded? Refunds come out of net sales. Where Gumroad keeps a processing fee on a refund, that fee comes out of Casa Scripta's share only.

Do you promise a certain income? No. Sales depend on whether your audience wants the book. There is no minimum and no guarantee.
