THE LICENCE

The terms, in plain English.

Who it's for

One named professional. If several people in your practice want to share the guides, each needs their own licence.

How long

12 months from the day you buy. You can buy again to keep sharing after that.

What you can do

Give as many copies as you like to your own clients, by email or printed, at no charge to them.

What you can't do

Sell the guides, post them publicly, put your own branding on them, or edit them. The guides stay exactly as published.
