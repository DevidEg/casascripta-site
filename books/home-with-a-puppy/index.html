Home with a Puppy: A week-by-week plan for the first 12 weeks, covering potty training, biting, crate and socialization

This book gives you a 3-day homecoming set-up and a 12-week plan. By the end, you'll have a daily routine that fits your home. You'll have clear steps for toilet trips, biting, the crate and time alone. And you'll have a record of the new things your puppy has met.

Who it's for, and who it isn't

Home with a Puppy is a week-by-week plan for the first 12 weeks, covering potty training, biting, crate and socialization.

Twelve weeks is the length of the plan, not a promise. Every puppy is different, and results vary. This is general information only. It is not veterinary advice, and it doesn't replace your vet. Check the red flags in Worksheet 2 before you start. In an emergency, call your vet or the nearest emergency vet clinic now.
