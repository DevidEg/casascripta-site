Who it's for, and who it isn't

This guide is for apartment dog owners whose dog howls the moment they leave, and whose neighbors have started to notice. It offers a week-one damage-control plan, a gentle, force-free 4-week foundation, and a path for the months after. Results vary; many dogs need months.

It is not veterinary advice, not legal advice, and not a substitute for a vet or a qualified behavior professional who can meet your dog. It does not name, recommend or discuss any medication, supplement or calming product. See a vet before you start if your dog hurts themselves trying to get out, tries to escape, won't eat or drink when alone, has accidents in the house, changes suddenly, or shows signs of pain. In an emergency, contact your nearest emergency vet. None of the organizations cited has reviewed or endorsed the book.
