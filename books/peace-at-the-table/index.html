Who it's for, and who it isn't

Peace at the Table is a no-pressure plan for picky eating and food refusal, for children aged 1 to 5.

Six weeks is the length of the plan, not a promise. Every child is different, and results vary. This is general information only. It is not medical advice, and it doesn't replace your child's doctor, nurse or dietitian. Check the red flags in Worksheet 2 before you start. In an emergency, call 911 in the US or Canada, 999 in the UK, or 000 in Australia.
