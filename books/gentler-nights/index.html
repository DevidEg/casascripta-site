Who it's for, and who it isn't

This guide is for parents whose nights got harder around 4 months and who want one calm, safe plan to follow for 14 nights, without leaving their baby to cry. It has a gentle-foundations track from about 4 months, and a gradual, parent-present step-back method from about 6 months, after checking with your pediatrician.

It is not medical advice, and it does not replace advice from your pediatrician, doctor, health visitor or child health nurse. 14 nights is the length of the plan, not a promised result; every baby is different. Follow safe-sleep rules for every sleep. Call your doctor or your local emergency number if the red-flag signs in the book apply — in an emergency, that is 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, or 000 in Australia.
