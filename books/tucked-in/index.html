Who it's for, and who it isn't

Tucked In is a calm plan for bedtime battles, night waking and early rising, for children aged 18 months to 4.

Fourteen nights is the length of the plan, not a promise. Every child is different, and results vary. This is general information only. It is not medical advice, and it doesn't replace your child's doctor or nurse. Check the red flags in Worksheet 2 before you start. In an emergency, call 911 in the US or Canada, 999 in the UK, or 000 in Australia.
